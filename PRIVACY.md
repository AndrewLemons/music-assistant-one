# Privacy Policy for Music Assistant One

**Effective date:** October 20, 2026

Music Assistant One is published by Lemony Click, LLC. The app connects to a Music Assistant server that you choose. The app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and we do not sell app data. The server you choose receives the information needed to sign you in and provide music and playback controls.

## Information on your device

To make the app work, it stores your server address, playback preferences, a limited cache of music library information, a local playback queue, downloaded audio, and associated artwork on your device. Upcoming songs may be cached automatically on Wi-Fi. Explicit downloads stay until you remove them; listening-cache files are removed as space is needed. Downloaded music and artwork are excluded from device backups. Access tokens and local-player pairing information are stored in the device's Keychain. If you sign in with a username and password, the password is used to request a token from your chosen server and is not saved by the app.

You can use **Settings → Server → Disconnect** to delete that server's saved access token, address, metadata, downloads, and listening cache. **Library → Downloaded** lets you remove individual downloads, and **Settings → Storage & Downloads** can clear the listening cache. To remove all local app data, use **Settings → Local App Data → Erase Local App Data**. This deletes saved server addresses, access tokens, audio and artwork, library caches, playback preferences, and this device's player identity and pairing records. Neither action deletes information on your Music Assistant server or with your music providers. We have no copy of your local data to access or delete.

## Connections you choose

The app sends sign-in details, playback commands, and requests for library information to your chosen Music Assistant server. It may load album artwork from that server or from an artwork URL provided by it. The optional audio companion runs on your chosen server and prepares full-track files for independent playback and downloads. It uses your existing server account and does not send your music to Lemony Click. Temporary track access tickets and provider stream details are not saved in the app's library. System background downloads may continue while the app is inactive. These connections are necessary to provide the features you use. Your server, its administrator, and any music providers connected to it may handle information under their own privacy practices.

We operate a limited Music Assistant instance for App Review with a dedicated review account and sample music. Requests to that instance may appear in its server and hosting logs. The review instance is not offered as a public account service. For any other server, its operator controls the account and server data.

## Changes and questions

If our practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. For questions about this policy, contact us through the [Music Assistant One project](https://github.com/AndrewLemons/music-assistant-one/issues). Please do not post personal information in a public issue.
