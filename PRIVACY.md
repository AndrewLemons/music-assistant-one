# Privacy Policy for Music Assistant One

**Effective date:** September 23, 2026

Music Assistant One is published by Lemony Click, LLC. The app connects to a Music Assistant server that you choose. **We do not collect personal information or app usage data from the app.** We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and we do not sell or share app data.

## Information on your device

To make the app work, it stores your server address, playback preferences, and a limited cache of music library information on your device. Access tokens and local-player pairing information are stored in the device's Keychain. If you sign in with a username and password, the password is used to request a token from your chosen server and is not saved by the app.

You can use **Disconnect and Choose Another Server** in the app to delete that server's saved access token, address, and library cache. Other preferences and the local-player pairing record may remain on your device. We have no copy of this information to access or delete.

## Connections you choose

The app sends sign-in details, playback commands, and requests for library information to your chosen Music Assistant server. It may load album artwork from that server or from an artwork URL provided by it. These connections are necessary to provide the features you use. Your server, its administrator, and any music providers connected to it may handle information under their own privacy practices. We do not operate or control those services.

## Changes and questions

If our practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. For questions about this policy, contact us through the [Music Assistant One project](https://github.com/AndrewLemons/music-assistant-one/issues). Please do not post personal information in a public issue.
